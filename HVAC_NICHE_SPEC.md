# HVAC Niche Specification — Antigravity AI

## Overview
This niche configuration specializes the Antigravity AI platform for **small HVAC companies (1–25 employees)** that suffer revenue loss due to missed calls, after-hours emergencies, and slow response times.

The system positions Antigravity AI as a **24/7 HVAC AI receptionist** that captures, qualifies, and escalates only high-value and urgent leads.

---

## Core Pain Points
- Missed inbound calls
- After-hours emergency requests
- Slow lead response times
- Owner overload and burnout
- Lost revenue from unanswered phones

---

## Ideal Customer Profile
- Small, owner-operated HVAC businesses
- Local service areas
- High seasonal demand (summer & winter)
- High-value emergency jobs

---

## AI Receptionist Behavior
**Tone:** Calm, confident, reassuring  
**Priority:** Speed and clarity  
**Primary Goal:** Capture and qualify leads  
**Secondary Goal:** Reduce unnecessary owner interruptions  

The AI receptionist must always:
- Answer immediately
- Calm distressed callers
- Ask structured HVAC-specific questions
- Escalate only true emergencies

---

## Qualification Flow
1. AC vs Heating
2. System completely down vs partially working
3. Emergency today? (Yes/No)
4. City/location
5. Callback phone number

---

## Escalation Rules
Owner is alerted if:
- Caller confirms emergency
- System is not working at all

Alerts are sent via SMS and dashboard notification.

---

## Outreach Positioning
Messaging focuses on:
- Lost revenue from missed calls
- After-hours emergencies
- Immediate ROI

No demos required. Demo Mode and AI Call Simulator handle proof.

---
